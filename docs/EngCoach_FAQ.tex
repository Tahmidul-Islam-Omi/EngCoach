\section{Access Mode}
Android App (with OTP verification over SMS).

\section{App Description}
EngCoach is a Bangla-medium English learning application. A short check first finds
which grammar rules and vocabulary the user is weak in, the lessons then cover only
those, and a second check measures the improvement. Two sections are live: Grammar
(6 topics) and Vocabulary (4 adaptive levels). Every rule, word and wrong answer is
explained in Bangla. Access requires an active subscription, confirmed through OTP
verification.

\section{How to Subscribe}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{OTP (Android App):} The user opens the EngCoach app and enters their
        Robi/Cirkle mobile number. An OTP is sent to that number by SMS. Entering the
        OTP in the app confirms and activates the subscription.
  \item \textbf{SMS:} The user types \textbf{engcoach} and sends it to
        \textbf{21213} to complete the subscription.
\end{itemize}

\section{How to Unsubscribe}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{SMS:} The user types \textbf{STOP engcoach} and sends it to \textbf{21213}.
  \item \textbf{In-App:} The user taps \textbf{Unsubscribe} on the Profile screen. Upon
        unsubscription the user is automatically logged out and returned to the login page.
\end{itemize}

\section{How to Use (User Manual)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Open the landing page and tap \textbf{Download App}, then install the APK.
  \item Open the EngCoach app and enter your Robi/Cirkle mobile number.
  \item Enter the OTP received by SMS to activate your subscription (2.78 BDT/day,
        incl.\ VAT+SC+SD).
  \item Tap \textbf{Learn}, choose \textbf{Grammar} or \textbf{Vocabulary}, and take the
        short check that finds your weak areas.
  \item Study the lessons your check produced, practise them, then take the second
        check to see your improvement.
  \item To cancel, go to \textbf{Profile} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Unsubscribe}, or send
        \textbf{STOP engcoach} to 21213.
\end{enumerate}
